Technical Guardrails

Hardcoded Lessons from Actual Railway Contracts

1. Compressor Wattage (Moradabad Precedent)

Northern Railway Moradabad rejected 29 water coolers because a vendor fitted 200W refrigerator compressors. For 150 LPH cooling duty under IS 1475:2001, the compressor must be rated ≥ 1550W using R134A refrigerant.

2. Inspection Precedence (Jaipur Precedent)

Under GeM GTC Clause 4, system-generated fields ("Pre-dispatch: NO") legally override conflicting buyer ATC text. When RITES is mandatory, factory pre-dispatch inspection must occur at Hapur before loading.

3. Statutory MSME Purchase Preference

Cool Home is 100% exempt from EMD and past turnover. If a non-MSE is L1 and Cool Home is within L1 + 15%, Cool Home has the statutory right to match L1 for 25% of the tender quantity.
