# RITES RE-INSPECTION REQUIREMENTS
## After Compressor Change - Do You Need Fresh RITES Inspection?

---

## SHORT ANSWER: YES, YOU NEED FRESH RITES INSPECTION

**When you change the compressor (from 200W to 1550W+), you are providing NEW material that requires fresh RITES inspection.**

---

## 1. WHY FRESH INSPECTION IS REQUIRED

### A. Material Change = New Product

**From RITES Guidelines:**
> "In case of issue of Rejection of material, fresh inspection call shall be accompanied with applicable miscellaneous charges."

**When you change compressor:**
- You are providing NEW units (not the rejected ones)
- New compressor = Different product specification
- Must be inspected as fresh material

### B. Original Inspection Invalid

**Original RITES Certificate:**
- Certificate No: N/NR/N26040339/ANEE
- Date: 20/07/2026
- Valid for: 29 units with 200W compressor
- **NOW INVALID** - You're providing different units

### C. Contract Requirement

**From Bid Document, Clause 3:**
> "Pre-dispatch Inspection at Seller Premises: RITES"
> "Post Receipt Inspection at consignee site before acceptance of stores: at consignee destination"

**Both inspections required for new material!**

---

## 2. THE RE-INSPECTION PROCESS

### Step 1: Remove Rejected Units

**From Indian Railway Standard Conditions of Contract, Clause 2201:**
> "On rejection of all stores submitted for inspection at a place other than the premises of the Contractor, such stores shall be removed by the Contractor at his own cost... within 21 days of the date of intimation of such rejection."

**Action:**
1. Remove 29 rejected units within 21 days
2. Avoid ground rent liability
3. Document removal with consignee

### Step 2: Source Correct Compressor

**Requirements:**
- Compressor rating: 1550W+ (or as per IS 1475 table)
- Application: Water cooler (not refrigerator)
- Type: Suitable for 150 L/hr cooling capacity
- Refrigerant: R134A (as per tender)

### Step 3: Register Fresh RITES Inspection Call

**From RITES Guidelines:**
> "Only On-line inspection call is accepted with minimum of seven clear working days from contract delivery period."

**Process:**
1. Log in to RITES vendor portal: http://ritesinsp.com/rbs/Vendor/Vendor_Login_Form.aspx
2. Register fresh inspection call for new units
3. Submit required documents

### Step 4: Submit Required Documents

**From RITES Guidelines:**
> "Complete and Original PO along with all amendments and other documents issued by purchaser (e.g. QAP, urgency letter, approved drawing etc.) till the date of initiation of inspection for the verification by IE."

**Documents Required:**
1. Original PO with amendments
2. Product data sheet with new compressor specs
3. Test reports for new compressor
4. ISI Mark certificate (if applicable)
5. Internal quality check reports

### Step 5: RITES Inspection at Seller Premises

**RITES Will Verify:**
1. Compressor specifications match PO
2. Cooling capacity performance
3. ISI Mark compliance
4. Material quality
5. Overall construction

### Step 6: Dispatch After RITES Clearance

**From RITES Guidelines:**
> "Vendor will not dispatch the material without receipt of valid Inspection Certificate."

**Only after RITES IC:**
- Dispatch units to consignee
- Submit bill with IC and dispatch documents

### Step 7: Consignee Inspection

**At Consignee Site:**
1. Sameer Mathur (SSE/ACSP/MB) inspects
2. Verifies against specifications
3. Issues acceptance/rejection

## 3. RITES INSPECTION CHARGES

### From RITES IVRS Document:

| Charge Type | Amount |
|-------------|--------|
| **Inspection Fee** | 0.9% of offered material value + GST @18% |
| **Re-inspection Charges** | 0.9% of offered material value, minimum Rs.5,000 + GST @18% |
| **Call Cancellation (Before IE Visit)** | 0.45% of offered value, max Rs.11,000 + GST @18% |
| **Call Cancellation (After IE Visit)** | 0.9% of offered value, max Rs.22,000 + GST @18% |
| **Revalidation of IC** | Rs.10,000 + GST @18% |

### Cost Calculation for This Case:

**Material Value:** Rs. 10,20,800 (29 units × Rs. 35,200)

**Inspection Fee:**
- 0.9% × Rs. 10,20,800 = Rs. 9,187
- + GST @18% = Rs. 1,654
- **Total: Rs. 10,841**

**Re-inspection Charges:**
- 0.9% × Rs. 10,20,800 = Rs. 9,187
- Minimum Rs. 5,000 (but 9,187 > 5,000)
- + GST @18% = Rs. 1,654
- **Total: Rs. 10,841**

**Note:** These charges are typically borne by the vendor (supplier)

## 5. WHAT RITES WILL CHECK

### A. Compressor Verification

**RITES Will Verify:**
1. Compressor model and specifications
2. Power rating (must be 1550W+ or as per IS 1475)
3. Application type (water cooler, not refrigerator)
4. Refrigerant type (R134A)
5. Manufacturer details

### B. Performance Testing

**RITES May Conduct:**
1. Cooling capacity test (150 L/hr verification)
2. Power consumption test (≤1550W)
3. Temperature drop test (30°C to 13.5°C)
4. COP calculation

### C. Documentation Check

**RITES Will Verify:**
1. Product data sheet matches PO
2. ISI Mark certificate (if required)
3. Test reports from certified lab
4. Material certificates

### D. Physical Inspection

**RITES Will Check:**
1. Construction quality
2. Material (SS 304 vs SS 202)
3. Insulation quality
4. Overall finish

## 7. RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: RITES Rejects Again

**Mitigation:**
1. Test thoroughly before RITES visit
2. Ensure compressor meets IS 1475
3. Have all documentation ready
4. Be present during inspection

### Risk 2: Consignee Rejects Again

**Mitigation:**
1. Request consignee to be present during RITES inspection
2. Get written confirmation of specifications
3. Test at consignee premises if possible
4. Document everything

### Risk 3: Timeline Delays

**Mitigation:**
1. Start process immediately
2. Source compressor in parallel
3. Register RITES call early
4. Keep buffer time

## 8. ALTERNATIVE APPROACH

### Can You Skip RITES Inspection?

**NO - Not Recommended:**

1. **Contract Requirement:** Bid specifies RITES pre-dispatch inspection
2. **Quality Assurance:** RITES verifies compliance
3. **Legal Protection:** RITES IC protects against future rejection
4. **Buyer Confidence:** Consignee trusts RITES certification

### Exception: If Consignee Agrees

**Possible if:**
1. Consignee waives RITES inspection (unlikely)
2. Consignee conducts inspection themselves
3. Written agreement from buyer

**Not Recommended:**
- Risk of rejection without RITES backing
- No third-party certification
- Legal complications
